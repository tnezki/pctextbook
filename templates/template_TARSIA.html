🧩 Tarsia Puzzle
Tarsia Puzzle
Setup: Groups of 4. Each group gets one handout and a pair of scissors. Cut out all the pieces before you begin.
  1. Examine the Pieces: Each piece has expressions, answers, or steps along its edges. Your job is to match edges that belong together.
  2. Build the Puzzle: Place pieces so that matching edges touch. When all pieces are correctly matched, they will form a complete shape.
  3. Check Your Shape: If the pieces form a clean shape with no gaps or overlaps, your matches are correct. If anything doesn't fit, there is an error somewhere — find it and fix it.
  4. Troubleshoot Together: If the shape doesn't close, go back and check each match. Work the math, don't just try random placements.
  5. When Finished: Raise your hand for the teacher to verify, then discuss: were there any matches that surprised you?
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Every match is verified by working the math, not guessing
  • Group catches a wrong match by checking the shape, not asking the teacher
  • All four members can explain why each edge pair belongs together
✗ What to Avoid
  • Forcing pieces to fit without checking the math
  • One person assembling while others watch
  • Calling it done before the shape is fully closed and clean
