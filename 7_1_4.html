Limits and Continuity
7-47

Evaluate each of the following expressions.

  1. \(\lim_{x\to 3} \frac{x^2-9}{x-3}\)

  2. \(\lim_{x\to -4} \frac{\sin(x)}{x}\)

  3. \(f(3)\text{ if } f(x)=\frac{x^2-9}{x-3}\)

  4. \(g\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\text{ if } g(x)=\frac{\sin(x)}{x}\)

  5. Compare your answers to parts (a) and (c) and parts (b) and (d). What can you conclude about \(f\) and \(g\)? Explain.
