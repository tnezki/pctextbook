9.D Solve Polynomial Equations

Round Table

Round Table Directions
1. Setup: Groups of 4. One whiteboard and marker per group.

2. Take Turns: Each student completes ONE step, then passes the board.

3. Continue: Rotate until the problem is complete.

4. Check: Compare when the solution is shown.

5. Celebrate: If everyone understands, celebrate.
Problem 1
Solve:
\( x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \)
Solution 1
Factor: \( (x-2)(x-3)=0 \)

\( x=2, 3 \)
Problem 2
Solve:
\( x^2 - 9 = 0 \)
Solution 2
\( (x-3)(x+3)=0 \)

\( x=±3 \)
Problem 3

Solution 3
\( x=0, 4 \)
Problem 4
Solve:
\( x^2 + x - 6 = 0 \)
Solution 4

Problem 5
Factor and solve:
\( x^3 - 3x^2 - 4x = 0 \)
Solution 5
\( x(x^2 - 3x - 4)=0 \)
\( (x-4)(x+1) \)

\( x=0,4,-1 \)
Problem 6
Solve:
\( x^4 - 4x^2 = 0 \)
Solution 6
\( x^2(x^2-4)=0 \)

\( x=0,2,-2 \)
Problem 7
Solve using grouping:
\( x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0 \)
Solution 7
\( (x^2(x-1) -4(x-1)) \)
\( (x-1)(x^2-4) \)

\( x=1,2,-2 \)
Problem 8
Solve:
\( x^3 + 2x^2 - x - 2 = 0 \)
Solution 8
\( (x^2(x+2) -1(x+2)) \)
\( (x+2)(x^2-1) \)

\( x=-2,1,-1 \)
