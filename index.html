Precalculus Curriculum

First and Second Semester Learning Targets

First Semester Topics

Module 1. Foundational Skills

  • 1.A Recognize functions and evaluate expressions
  • 1.B Simplify expressions with exponents and radicals
  • 1.C Apply basic trigonometry to right triangles
  • 1.D Apply trigonometry to real world problems
  • 1.E Calculate average rate of change

Module 2. Solving and Systems

  • 2.A Write the equation of a line
  • 2.B Solve linear and quadratic equations
  • 2.C Solve linear systems of equations
  • 2.D Solve nonlinear systems of equations
  • 2.E Write and simplify average rate of change expressions

Module 3. Function Operations, Inverses, and Piecewise

  • 3.A Complete operations with functions
  • 3.B Write equations of inverse functions
  • 3.C Graph and evaluate piecewise-defined functions
  • 3.D Transform functions
  • 3.E Simplify and manipulate the difference quotient

Module 4. Function Analysis and Domain/Range

  • 4.A Identify domain and range from graphs
  • 4.B Describe functions and their properties
  • 4.C Identify even and odd functions
  • 4.D State end behavior of functions
  • 4.E Limit as x approaches infinity

Module 5. Unit Circle and Trigonometric Identities

  • 5.A Work with radians, degrees, and unit circle
  • 5.B Determine sine, cosine, tangent of special angles
  • 5.C Graph sinusoidal functions
  • 5.D Solve trigonometric equations
  • 5.E Optimization problems

Second Semester Topics

Module 6. Polynomial Functions and Equations

  • 6.A Graph quadratic equations
  • 6.B Sketch graphs of polynomial functions
  • 6.C Connect intercepts to factors
  • 6.D Determine key features of polynomials
  • 6.E Use sigma notation

Module 7. Trigonometric Solutions and Identities

  • 7.A Determine sine, cosine, tangent of special angles
  • 7.B Apply double-angle and Pythagorean identities
  • 7.C Apply half-angle identity
  • 7.D Solve advanced trigonometric equations
  • 7.E Approximate area under the curve

Module 8. Polynomial and Rational Expressions

  • 8.A Factor expressions
  • 8.B Complete operations with rational expressions
  • 8.C Simplify complex fractions
  • 8.D Divide polynomials
  • 8.E Evaluate limits from a graph

Module 9. Rational Functions and Asymptotes

  • 9.A Graph transformations of rational functions
  • 9.B Identify asymptotes
  • 9.C Graph reciprocal functions
  • 9.D Solve and graph polynomial equations
  • 9.E Evaluate limits from expressions

Module 10. Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

  • 10.A Rewrite expressions using logarithm properties
  • 10.B Graph transformations of logarithmic functions
  • 10.C Solve equations with exponents and logarithms
  • 10.D Solve logarithm functions with extraneous solutions
  • 10.E Derivative: Power Rule
