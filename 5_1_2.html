Stretching Exponential Functions

For each of the following functions, compare the graph of \(y=g(x)\) to the graph of \(f(x)=x^2\). Describe the transformation using words.

  1. \(g(x)=3x^2\)

  1. \(h(x)=(3x)^2\)

  1. \(g(x)=\left(\frac{1}{4}x\right)^2\)
