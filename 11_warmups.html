11.1 Warm Up 2

Complete the four problems below. Show your work in the space provided.

1. Find the derivative of $f(x)=x^7-4x^2$.

2. Rewrite and then differentiate $g(x)=3\sqrt{x}-\dfrac{2}{x^3}$.

3. Find the derivative of $h(x)=5\sin x-2\cos x$.

4. Find the derivative of $p(x)=4x^3+3\sin x-\cos x$.

11.1 Warm Up 3

These four problems are a little more challenging. Show all steps clearly.

1. Find the derivative of $f(x)=6x^{5/2}-3x^{-4}$.

2. Find the derivative of $g(x)=7\sin x+4\cos x-2x^{1/3}$.

3. Find the derivative of $h(x)=2\sqrt{x}+5x^3-4\cos x$.

4. If $q(x)=3x^4+2\sin x-\cos x$, find $q'(x)$ and then evaluate $q'(0)$.

11.2 Warm Up 2

Decide when the product rule is needed, and use it when appropriate.

1. Decide whether the product rule is needed for each expression.

  • $x^2+\sin x$
  • $(x^2+1)\sin x$

2. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}(x\sin x)$.

3. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}(x\cos x)$.

4. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}\big((x^2+1)\sin x\big)$.

11.2 Warm Up 3

These four problems use more involved product-rule structure.

1. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}\big((3x^2-x+1)\sin x\big)$.

2. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}\big(\cos x(x^3+2x)\big)$.

3. If $q(x)=(x^2-x+3)\sin x$, find $q'(x)$ and then evaluate $q'(0)$.

4. Explain why $\dfrac{d}{dx}\big((x^2+1)\sin x\big)$ is not equal to $(2x)(\cos x)$.

11.3 Warm Up 2

Use the product rule, then connect slope and point to the tangent line.

1. For $f(x)=(x+1)\sin x$, find $f'(0)$.

2. Find the point on the graph of $f(x)=(x+1)\sin x$ when $x=0$.

3. Write the equation of the tangent line to $f(x)=(x+1)\sin x$ at $x=0$.

4. In one or two sentences, explain what the slope of that tangent line says about the function at $x=0$.

11.3 Warm Up 3

These four problems are more advanced tangent-line questions from Section 11.3.

1. Find the tangent line to $g(x)=(x^2+1)\sin x$ at $x=0$.

2. Find the tangent line to $h(x)=\cos x(x^2-1)$ at $x=0$.

3. Find the tangent line to $p(x)=(2x^2-x+1)\sin x$ at $x=0$.

4. Explain how the derivative and the original function play different roles in writing a tangent-line equation.
