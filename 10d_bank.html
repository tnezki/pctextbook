Level 5 — Conceptual & Justification

  1. Extraneous solutions occur when algebraic steps ignore domain restrictions.
  2. Logarithms require positive arguments, restricting possible values.
  3. Combining logs can introduce solutions not valid in the original equation.
  4. Quadratic structure appears when logs are combined or substituted.
  5. Checking ensures all solutions satisfy the original equation.
