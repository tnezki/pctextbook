Level 5 — Conceptual & Justification

  1. The product rule comes from exponent rules: multiplying numbers corresponds to adding exponents, so logs convert multiplication into addition.
  2. A coefficient represents repeated multiplication, which corresponds to an exponent inside the logarithm.
  3. Logarithms do not distribute over addition because exponent rules do not support splitting sums into products.
  4. Logarithm properties are direct inverses of exponent rules, such as \(a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}\).
  5. Rewriting using exponents allows fractional powers and roots to be handled using the power rule.
