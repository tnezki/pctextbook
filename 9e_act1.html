9.E Evaluate Limits

Solution Relay

Solution Relay Directions
1. Setup: Form a group of 4. Each person gets a blank piece of paper. The teacher will project a problem.

2. Plan (1 min): Write the steps or procedure needed to solve the problem.

3. Share (1 min): Discuss your procedure with your group.

4. Check: The teacher will ask groups to share their procedure, confirm correct approaches, and discuss alternative strategies.

5. Solve: Each student completes the problem individually, then checks with the group.

Goal: Build a complete solution by clearly explaining each step.
