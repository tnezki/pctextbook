Module 10: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions
Essential Standard
Students will rewrite expressions using properties of logarithms, graph transformations of exponential and logarithmic functions, solve exponential and logarithmic equations — including those with extraneous solutions — and extend learning by applying the Power Rule to find derivatives of polynomial functions.
Section 10.A: Rewrite Expressions Using the Properties of Logarithms
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Condense logarithmic expressions into a single logarithm by applying the product, quotient, and power properties.
•  Expand logarithmic expressions at Level 1 by applying the product, quotient, and power properties to rewrite a single logarithm.
•  Expand logarithmic expressions at Level 3, including complex expressions with roots and multiple factors, attending to precision.
Section 10.B: Graph Basic Transformations of the Family of Logarithmic Functions
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Identify and describe key features of exponential and logarithmic functions including domain, range, intercepts, and asymptotes.
•  Make a table of values, plot points, graph logarithmic functions at Level 1, and determine domain and range.
•  Graph exponential and logarithmic functions including transformations such as shifts, reflections, and stretches.
Section 10.C: Solve Equations with Exponents and Logarithms
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Solve exponential equations at Level 1 by rewriting both sides with a common base and applying properties of exponents.
•  Solve basic logarithmic equations by converting between logarithmic and exponential form.
•  Solve first-degree logarithmic equations by applying logarithm properties and isolating the variable.
Section 10.D: Solve Logarithmic Functions with Extraneous Solutions
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Solve second-degree logarithmic equations at Level 1 by condensing, converting to exponential form, and solving the resulting quadratic.
•  Solve second-degree logarithmic equations at Level 2, including complex cases, and check all solutions for extraneous values.
•  Solve natural logarithmic equations by applying properties of ln and the inverse relationship between e and the natural logarithm.
Above and Beyond: Extended Learning (Section 10.E)
Learning Objectives for Above and Beyond – I can…
•  Find derivatives of polynomial functions by applying the Power Rule to each term and simplifying.
•  Evaluate a derivative function at a given value to find the instantaneous rate of change at a specific point.
•  Apply the Power Rule at Level 2, including functions with fractional and negative exponents.
•  Apply the Power Rule at Level 3 to more complex polynomial and rational expressions, persevering through multi-step differentiation.
