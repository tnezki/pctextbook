Module 08: Polynomial and Rational Expressions
Essential Standard
Students will factor polynomial expressions, perform operations with rational expressions, simplify complex fractions, and divide polynomials, connecting algebraic structure to procedural fluency while applying mathematical practices to model real-world situations and extend learning through limits.
Section 8.A: Factor Expressions
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Factor trinomial expressions using structural patterns including leading coefficient recognition (Level 1 and Level 2).
•  Factor expressions with a binomial greatest common factor by identifying and extracting common polynomial factors.
•  Look for and use the structure of polynomial expressions to choose and apply the most efficient factoring strategy.
Section 8.B: Complete Operations with Rational Expressions
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Simplify rational expressions by factoring numerators and denominators and canceling common factors, attending to restrictions on the variable.
•  Multiply and divide rational expressions using factoring and cancellation, reasoning abstractly about equivalent forms.
•  Add and subtract rational expressions by finding common denominators and combining numerators precisely.
Section 8.C: Simplify Complex Fractions
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Add and subtract fractions containing variables by identifying least common denominators and simplifying results.
•  Multiply and divide fractions with variables, including using complex fraction structure as a division strategy.
•  Simplify complex fractions by rewriting them as division problems or using the LCD method, persevering through multi-step simplifications.
Section 8.D: Divide Polynomials
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Divide polynomials using long division, connecting the process to numerical long division and interpreting remainders.
•  Apply synthetic division as an efficient algorithm for dividing by linear binomials and verify using the box method.
•  Use the polynomial division box method to organize and confirm division results, looking for regularity across methods.
Above and Beyond: Extended Learning (Section 8.E)
Learning Objectives for Above and Beyond – I can…
•  Evaluate one-sided and two-sided limits from graphs, identifying left-hand and right-hand limit values at a point.
•  Classify discontinuities from graphs as continuous, infinite, jump, or removable, and express limits using formal notation for logarithmic and exponential functions.
•  Find limits from a table of values and make sense of limit behavior as inputs approach a value from either direction.
