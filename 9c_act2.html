9.C Rational Functions

Traveling Salesman

Traveling Salesman Directions
1. Setup: Groups of 4. One whiteboard and marker per group.

2. Solve: Work together to solve the problem and prepare an explanation.

3. Rotate: One student travels to a new group and presents the solution.

4. Listen: Receiving group listens, asks questions, and checks understanding.

5. Repeat: Teacher gives a new problem. Groups solve again.
Problem 1
Find x-intercepts:
\( f(x)=\frac{x-4}{x+2} \)
Answer
x=4
Problem 2
Find y-intercept:
\( f(x)=\frac{x+3}{x-2} \)
Answer
y=-\(\frac{3}{2}\)
Problem 3
Determine if x-intercept exists:
\( f(x)=\frac{x^2+4}{x+1} \)
Answer
none
Problem 4
Find intercepts:
\( f(x)=\frac{x(x+1)}{x-3} \)
Answer
x=0,-1; y=0
Problem 5

\( f(x)=\frac{x-5}{x-2} \)
Answer
x=5; VA=2
