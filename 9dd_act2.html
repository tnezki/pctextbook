Stations Directions
1. Get record sheet (8 stations)

2. Pick a station to start (no more than 4 students)

3. You have 3 minutes per station

4. Rotate when teacher says
(YOU CAN’T ALL GO TO THE SAME NEW STATION)
STATION 1
Solve:
\( x^2 - 6x + 8 = 0 \)
\( x^2 - 16 = 0 \)
\( x(x-5)=0 \)
\( x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0 \)
STATION 2
Factor and solve:
\( x^3 - 5x^2 - 6x = 0 \)
\( x^4 - 9x^2 = 0 \)
\( x^3 + 3x^2 - 4x - 12 = 0 \)
\( x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0 \)
STATION 3
Solve using synthetic division:
\( x^3 - 2x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \)
\( x^3 - 4x^2 + x + 6 = 0 \)
\( x^3 + x^2 - 10x - 8 = 0 \)
STATION 4
Solve completely:
\( x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6 = 0 \)
\( x^4 - 6x^2 + 9 = 0 \)
STATION 5
Solve:
\( x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0 \)
\( x^2 - 25 = 0 \)
\( x(x-6)=0 \)
\( x^2 + x - 12 = 0 \)
STATION 6
Factor and solve:
\( x^3 - 4x^2 - 4x = 0 \)
\( x^4 - 16x^2 = 0 \)
\( x^3 + 2x^2 - 9x - 18 = 0 \)
\( x^4 + 2x^3 - x^2 - 2x = 0 \)
STATION 7
Solve:
\( x^3 - 3x^2 - 4x + 12 = 0 \)
\( x^3 - 5x^2 + 4x + 12 = 0 \)
