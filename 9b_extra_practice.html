Level 1 — Basic Asymptotes

Identify Vertical Asymptotes

Directions: Identify the vertical asymptote.

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{1}{x-4} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{3}{x+6} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{5}{x-2} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{2}{x+3} \)

Identify Horizontal Asymptotes

Directions: Identify the horizontal asymptote.

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{3x}{x^2+1} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{7}{x^3+4} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{4x^2}{x^2+5} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{8x^2}{4x^2+1} \)

Determine Existence

Directions: Determine whether a horizontal asymptote exists.
