Module 03: Function Operations, Inverses, and Piecewise Functions
Essential Standard
Students will perform operations with functions including composition, write and interpret inverse functions algebraically and graphically, graph and evaluate piecewise-defined functions, and apply graphing form y = a(b(x − h))^n + k to transform parent functions including linear, quadratic, cubic, radical, cube root, absolute value, and reciprocal functions.
Section 3.A: Complete Operations with Functions, Including Composition
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Evaluate and simplify compositions of functions by substituting one function into another and simplifying the result.
•  Find and simplify the composition of functions written in terms of x, including (f ° g)(x) and (g ° f)(x), attending to order.
•  Perform and interpret function operations and compositions from mixed representations including equations, tables, and graphs.
Section 3.B: Write the Equation of the Inverse of a Function or Determine if Two Functions Are Inverses
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Find the inverse of a linear function by switching x and y and solving for y, and verify using composition.
•  Find the inverse of multi-step functions by applying inverse operations in reverse order and simplifying.
•  Determine whether two functions are inverses graphically by identifying reflection symmetry over the line y = x.
Section 3.C: Graph and/or Evaluate Piecewise-Defined Functions
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Evaluate piecewise-defined functions by identifying the correct piece for a given input value and substituting.
•  Graph piecewise-defined functions at Level 1 by graphing each piece over its restricted domain with correct endpoints.
•  Write the equation of a piecewise-defined function from a graph at Level 1 by identifying each piece’s rule and domain restriction.
Section 3.D: Transform Functions
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Apply graphing form y = a(b(x − h))^n + k to graph transformations of parent functions including linear, quadratic, cubic, radical, cube root, absolute value, and reciprocal functions.
•  Identify and describe the effect of stretch and compression parameters on the shape of a function’s graph.
•  Graph transformations of non-standard function shapes, including W-type and other non-parent forms, by applying transformation rules systematically.
Above and Beyond: Extended Learning (Section 3.E)
Learning Objectives for Above and Beyond – I can…
•  Simplify the difference quotient for a given function by substituting x+h, expanding, and fully simplifying the expression.
•  Interpret secant line limits graphically by connecting the behavior of the difference quotient as h approaches zero to the slope of the tangent line.
•  Evaluate secant line limits numerically by computing difference quotient values for smaller and smaller values of h and identifying the limiting value.
•  Draw tangent lines to curves at a given point and estimate instantaneous rate of change from the slope of the tangent line.
