Level 1 — Problem 1
Identify the vertical asymptote.
\( f(x)=\frac{1}{x-2} \)
Answer
VA: \(x=2\)
Level 1 — Problem 2
Identify the horizontal asymptote.
\( f(x)=\frac{3x}{x^2+1} \)
Answer
HA: \(y=0\)
Level 1 — Problem 3
Identify the horizontal asymptote.

Answer
HA: \(y=2\)

Find all vertical asymptotes.

Answer

Level 2 — Problem 5
Find the horizontal asymptote.

Answer

Identify all asymptotes.

Answer
VA: \(x=\pm1\), HA: \(y=2\)
Level 3 — Problem 8
Find all asymptotes.
\( f(x)=\frac{x}{x^2-5x+6} \)
Answer
VA: \(x=2,3\), HA: \(y=0\)
Level 3 — Problem 9
Simplify and identify asymptotes.
\( \frac{x-2}{(x-2)(x+1)} \)
Answer
Hole at \(x=2\), VA: \(x=-1\), HA: \(y=0\)
Level 3 — Problem 10
Determine all asymptotes.
\( f(x)=\frac{3x^2}{x^2+4} \)
Answer
HA: \(y=3\)
Level 4 — Problem 11
Find the slant asymptote.
\( f(x)=\frac{x^2+1}{x} \)
Answer
SA: \(y=x\)
Level 4 — Problem 12
Find all asymptotes.
\( f(x)=\frac{x^2+1}{x-2} \)
Answer
VA: \(x=2\), SA: \(y=x+2\)
Level 4 — Problem 13
Find all asymptotes.
\( f(x)=\frac{2x^2-3}{x+1} \)
Answer
VA: \(x=-1\), SA: \(y=2x-2\)
Level 5 — Problem 14
Explain why horizontal asymptotes depend only on leading terms.
Answer
Leading terms dominate behavior as \(x \to \infty\), so lower terms become negligible.
Level 5 — Problem 15
Explain why a slant asymptote occurs when degrees differ by one.
Answer
Division produces a linear expression plus a remainder that approaches zero.
