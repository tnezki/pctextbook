EXTREMA

Points on a graph that are “high” points or “low” points are called extreme points, or extrema. A local maximum/minimum of a function is the largest/smallest value of the function within a small interval around the extreme point. A global maximum/minimum of a function is the largest/smallest value of the function over its entire domain. The plural of maximum is maxima and the plural of minimum is minima.

  1. Look back at the graph in problem 2-1 representing Riley’s drive. Which points are the maxima? Label each maximum point as local or global. What is happening in Riley’s drive at these points?

  2. Which points are the minima? Label each minimum point as local or global. What is happening in Riley’s drive at these points?

  3. Describe the graph near a local maximum point using the words increasing and decreasing. How is it changing?

  4. Describe the graph near a local minimum point using the words increasing and decreasing. How is it changing?
