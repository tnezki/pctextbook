Let \(g(x)=3x+\frac{12}{x}\) for \(x>0\).

  1. Sketch a graph of \(y=g(x)\), showing that the minimum value of the function occurs when \(x=2\).
  2. Calculate the slope of the line tangent to \(y=g(x)\) when \(x=2\).

Steady Freddy always drives his car at a constant rate of \(30\) miles per hour.

  1. Write an equation for Freddy’s velocity as a function of time. Sketch a graph of this function.
  2. How far has Freddy traveled after \(1\) hour? After \(2\) hours? After \(3\) hours?
  3. Using your answers to part (b), what is Freddy’s position as a function of time?
  4. How is Freddy’s position function related to the graph of Freddy’s velocity function?
  5. How is Freddy’s velocity function related to his position function?
