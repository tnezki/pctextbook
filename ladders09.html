Module 09: Rational Functions and Asymptotes
Essential Standard
Students will graph transformations of rational functions, identify horizontal, vertical, and slant asymptotes algebraically, analyze reciprocal functions and their key features, and solve polynomial equations — connecting algebraic structure to graphical behavior while applying mathematical practices to model real-world situations and extend learning through limits of rational functions.
Section 9.A: Graph Transformations of Rational Functions
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Graph basic rational functions by identifying key features including domain, range, and asymptotic behavior from the equation.
•  Graph rational functions by applying transformations and analyzing how parameters affect the shape and position of the graph.
•  Determine key features of a rational graph including intercepts, asymptotes, holes, and end behavior from its equation.
Section 9.B: Identify Horizontal, Vertical, and Slant Asymptotes
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Find vertical asymptotes algebraically by identifying values that make the denominator zero after factoring and canceling.
•  Find horizontal asymptotes algebraically by comparing the degrees of the numerator and denominator polynomials.
•  Find slant asymptotes using polynomial division and determine whether a rational function has a horizontal or slant asymptote.
Section 9.C: Graph Reciprocal Functions and Identify Their Asymptotes
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Find x-intercepts of rational functions algebraically by setting the numerator equal to zero and solving.
•  Find y-intercepts of rational functions algebraically by substituting x = 0 and simplifying.
•  Determine a complete set of features of a rational graph at Level 2, including intercepts, asymptotes, holes, and behavior near asymptotes.
Section 9.D: Solve and Graph Polynomial Equations
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Find all integer roots of a polynomial equation using the Integer Root Theorem and synthetic or long division to factor completely.
•  Find all roots of a polynomial equation including irrational roots by applying the Integer Root Theorem and the quadratic formula.
•  Write the equation of a polynomial function from its graph by identifying roots, multiplicities, and the leading coefficient.
Above and Beyond: Extended Learning (Section 9.E)
Learning Objectives for Above and Beyond – I can…
•  Find holes in rational functions algebraically by identifying factors that cancel from numerator and denominator.
•  Write limits from a graph of a rational function using correct limit notation, including one-sided and two-sided limits at holes.
•  Evaluate limits of rational functions using direct substitution when the function is defined at the point.
•  Evaluate limits of rational functions at holes by factoring, canceling, and substituting to find the limiting value.
