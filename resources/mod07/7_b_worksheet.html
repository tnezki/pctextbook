7.B Practice

Double-Angle & Pythagorean Identities

1. If $\cos\theta=\frac{5}{13}$ and $\theta$ is in Quadrant I, find $\sin(2\theta)$.
2. If $\sin\theta=\frac{4}{5}$ and $\theta$ is in Quadrant II, find $\cos(2\theta)$.
3. Rewrite $\sin(2\theta)$ entirely in terms of $\cos\theta$.
4. Rewrite $\cos(2\theta)$ entirely in terms of $\tan\theta$.
5. Simplify $\frac{1+\cos(2\theta)}{2}$.
6. Simplify $\frac{1-\sin^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta}$.
7. Simplify $\frac{\cos(2\theta)+1}{\cos^2\theta}$.
8. If $\tan\theta=\frac{3}{4}$ and $\theta$ is in Quadrant I, find $\cos(2\theta)$.
9. Simplify $\frac{2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$.
10. If $\cos\theta=-\frac{7}{25}$ and $\theta$ is in Quadrant II, find $\sin(2\theta)$.
11. Simplify $\frac{1-\tan^2\theta}{\sec^2\theta}$.
12. Rewrite $\cos(2\theta)$ using only $\sec\theta$.
13. Simplify $\frac{\sin^2\theta-\cos^2\theta}{\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta}$.
14. If $\sin\theta=-\frac{8}{17}$ and $\theta$ is in Quadrant III, find $\cos(2\theta)$.
15. Simplify $\frac{1+\tan^2\theta-\sec^2\theta}{\cos(2\theta)}$.
16. Express $\sin(2\theta)$ entirely in terms of $\tan\theta$.

Solutions

1. $\frac{120}{169}$

2. $-\frac{7}{25}$

3. $2\cos\theta\sqrt{1-\cos^2\theta}$

4. $\frac{1-\tan^2\theta}{1+\tan^2\theta}$

5. $\cos^2\theta$

6. $1$

7. $2$

8. $\frac{7}{25}$

9. $2\cos\theta$

10. $-\frac{336}{625}$

11. $\cos(2\theta)\cos^2\theta$

12. $\frac{2-\sec^2\theta}{\sec^2\theta}$

13. $-\cos(2\theta)$

14. $\frac{161}{289}$

15. $0$

16. $\frac{2\tan\theta}{1+\tan^2\theta}$
