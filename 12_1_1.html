12.1.1 Is there an efficient method?

Arithmetic Series
12-10

Evaluate each of the following sums.

  1. \(3+6+9+\dots+90\)

  2. \(20+22+24+\dots+100\)

  3. \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^{41}(2n+18)\)

  4. \(\displaystyle \sum_{k=8}^{53}(5k-3)\)
