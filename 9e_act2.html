9.E Evaluate Limits

Numbered Heads Together

Directions
1. Form a group of 4. Each person gets a number (1–4).

2. Work together to solve the problem and make sure everyone understands the solution.

3. Be ready — the teacher will call a number.

4. The student with that number must explain the solution for the group.

Goal: Everyone understands every step.
Level 1
Evaluate:
1. \( \lim_{x \to 2} (x^2 + 4) \)
2. \( \lim_{x \to -1} (2x + 3) \)
3. \( \lim_{x \to 3} (x^2 - x + 2) \)
4. \( \lim_{x \to 0} (5 - x^2) \)
