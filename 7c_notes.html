SECTION 7.C
Half-Angle, Sum, and Difference Identities

Learning Objectives

  • I can apply half-angle formulas for sine and cosine to evaluate exact expressions.
  • I can apply half-angle formulas for tangent and determine appropriate signs.
  • I can apply angle sum and difference identities to rewrite and evaluate trigonometric expressions.
  • I can justify multi-step reasoning using algebraic structure.

Introduction

In Section 7.B, we rewrote trigonometric expressions involving double angles.

Now we extend that structure.

Instead of working with $2\theta$, we will:

  • Break angles apart
  • Combine angles strategically
  • Rewrite unfamiliar angles using known unit circle values

Structure matters.

Key Vocabulary

Half-Angle Formula: An identity that expresses a trig function of $\frac{\theta}{2}$ in terms of $\theta$.

Sum Identity: A formula that expresses a trig function of $A + B$.

Difference Identity: A formula that expresses a trig function of $A - B$.

Sign Determination: Deciding whether a half-angle expression is positive or negative based on quadrant.

Core Identities

Half-Angle

$\sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos\theta}{2}}$

$\cos\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos\theta}{2}}$

$\tan\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)=\frac{\sin\theta}{1+\cos\theta}$

Sum and Difference

$\sin(A+B)=\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B$

$\sin(A-B)=\sin A\cos B-\cos A\sin B$

$\cos(A+B)=\cos A\cos B-\sin A\sin B$

$\cos(A-B)=\cos A\cos B+\sin A\sin B$

Structure Insight

  • Half-angle formulas contain square roots, introducing $\pm$.
  • The sign depends on the quadrant of $\frac{\theta}{2}$.
  • Cosine switches signs between sum and difference forms.
  • Precision in signs is critical.

Examples

Example 1: Evaluate $\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{8}\right)$.
Example 2: Evaluate $\cos(75^\circ)$.
Example 3: Rewrite $\sin(45^\circ+30^\circ)$.
Example 4: Simplify $\cos(A-B)+\cos(A+B)$.

Summary

  • Half-angle identities introduce square roots and $\pm$ signs.
  • Signs depend on quadrant location.
  • Sum and difference identities rewrite unfamiliar angles.
  • Structure and precision are essential.

Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting the $\pm$ in half-angle formulas.
  • Using the wrong quadrant for sign.
  • Mixing up cosine sign patterns.
  • Not fully simplifying radicals.
