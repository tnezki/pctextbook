Module 07: Trigonometric Solutions and Identities
Essential Standard
Students will understand and apply trigonometric identities and advanced solution strategies, including unit circle reasoning, identity manipulation, equation solving in radians, and mathematical modeling through approximation methods, while engaging deeply in the Mathematical Practices.
Section 7.A: Exact Trigonometric Values
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Determine exact values of sine, cosine, and tangent for special angles on the unit circle.
•  Determine exact values of all six trigonometric functions in Quadrants II–IV using reference angles.
•  Interpret exact trigonometric values graphically in radians, including multiples of π.
Section 7.B: Double-Angle and Pythagorean Identities
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Apply double-angle identities for sine and cosine to evaluate and simplify expressions.
•  Simplify trigonometric expressions using Pythagorean identities, including rational expressions.
•  Recognize equivalent forms of identities, including negatives and multiples, and justify my reasoning.
Section 7.C: Half-Angle, Sum, and Difference Identities
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Apply half-angle formulas for sine and cosine to evaluate exact expressions.
•  Apply half-angle formulas for tangent and determine appropriate signs.
•  Apply angle sum and difference identities to rewrite and evaluate trigonometric expressions.
Section 7.D: Solving Advanced Trigonometric Equations
Learning Objectives – I can…
•  Solve basic trigonometric equations visually for all six trig functions.
•  Solve linear trigonometric equations in radians involving secant, cosecant, and cotangent.
•  Solve advanced trigonometric equations in radians without fractional forms and verify solutions.
Above and Beyond: Extended Learning
Learning Objectives for Above and Beyond – I can…
•  Approximate the area under a curve using left and right Riemann sums from graphs and tables.
•  Apply trapezoidal sums from tables and equations to approximate area.
•  Compare approximation methods and justify which method provides a better estimate.

Assessment Methods
Rung 1:Unit circle quizzes (non-π and π radians), exact value practice, and pattern recognition checks.
Rung 2:Reference angle tasks in Quadrants II–IV and precision-based exact value assessments.
Rung 3:Identity simplification tasks requiring justification of each algebraic step.
Rung 4:Multi-step identity proofs and expression rewrites using half-angle and sum/difference formulas.
Rung 5:Advanced trigonometric equation sets (including sec, csc, and cot) with graphical verification.
Rung 6:Riemann and trapezoidal sum activities from graphs and tables with modeling explanations.
Rung 7:Comparative analysis task evaluating approximation methods with written mathematical justification.
