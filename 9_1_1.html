Shola’s Bakery uses sugar, eggs, and butter in all of its cakes, as seen in the frosting. Matrix \(C\) shows how many eggs, cups of sugar, and ounces of butter are used in each type of fancy cake, and each chef’s food card. Matrix \(F\) shows how many eggs, cups of sugar, and ounces of butter are used in each type of cookie, and which food cards. Matrix \(L\) shows how much sugar, eggs, and butter Shola will need to fill Leora’s order.

\(C=\begin{bmatrix}9 & 8 & b \\ 6 & 1 & 5 \\ 3 & 1.5 & 4\end{bmatrix}\qquad F=\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 2 & 4\end{bmatrix}\qquad L=\begin{bmatrix}5 & 2\end{bmatrix}\)

  1. Write the matrix \(C+F\), being sure to label the rows and columns, and explain what it represents.

  2. Write the matrix \(3C\), with labels, and explain what it represents.

  3. Leora orders three angel food cakes and two devil’s food cakes with frosting, as represented by the matrix \(L\) at right. Use matrix multiplication to write a matrix that shows how much sugar, eggs, and butter Shola will need to fill Leora’s order. Show all of your work.

Juan loves to ride his bicycle. Today he is cruising along Exeter Street, which is on flat ground, at \(20\) miles per hour \((29.3\text{ feet per second})\). On his front wheel is a reflector \(11\) inches from the center of the wheel. Juan’s bike has \(26\) inch wheels. Assume that the reflector is furthest from the ground when the wheel is at \((0,26)\) in the wheels of the reflector above the ground (in inches), the elapsed time \((\text{in seconds})\) and the distance \((\text{in inches})\) Juan has traveled \(d\) in seconds.

  1. How far does Juan travel (in inches) when the wheel makes one complete revolution?

  2. Write an equation for \(h(d)\), the height of the reflector, in terms of the distance Juan has traveled \(d\).

  3. What is the height of the reflector after Juan rides \(20\) feet?

  4. Write an equation for \(h(t)\), the height of the reflector, in terms of the time \(t\).

  5. What is the height of the reflector after Juan has ridden for \(5\) minutes?
