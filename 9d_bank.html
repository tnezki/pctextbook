9.D Solve Polynomial Equations

Question Bank

Level 1 — Basic Factoring

Solve each equation.

  1. \( x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^2 - 9 = 0 \)
  3. \( x(x-4)=0 \)
  4. \( x^2 + x - 6 = 0 \)

Solve each equation.

  1. \( x^3 - 4x^2 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^3 - x^2 - 6x = 0 \)
  3. \( x(x+2)(x-3)=0 \)
  4. \( x^3 - 9x = 0 \)

Determine if a solution exists.

  1. \( x^2 + 1 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^4 + 4 = 0 \)
  3. \( x(x^2+1)=0 \)
  4. \( x^2 - 2x + 1 = 0 \)

Level 2 — Factoring + Structure

Factor and solve.

  1. \( x^3 - 3x^2 - 4x = 0 \)
  2. \( x^4 - 4x^2 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^3 + 2x^2 - x - 2 = 0 \)
  4. \( x^4 - x^3 - 6x^2 + 6x = 0 \)

Use grouping to solve.

  1. \( x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^3 + x^2 - 4x - 4 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^4 + 2x^3 - x^2 - 2x = 0 \)
  4. \( x^4 - 3x^3 - x^2 + 3x = 0 \)

Solve and list all solutions.

  1. \( x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^3 - 3x^2 + 3x - 1 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^4 - 6x^2 + 9 = 0 \)
  4. \( x^3 + x^2 - 9x - 9 = 0 \)

Level 3 — Synthetic Division

Use synthetic division to solve.

  1. \( x^3 - 2x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^3 - 4x^2 + x + 6 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^3 - 3x^2 - 4x + 12 = 0 \)
  4. \( x^3 + x^2 - 10x - 8 = 0 \)

Solve completely.

  1. \( x^4 - 2x^3 - 7x^2 + 8x + 12 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^4 - 5x^3 + 6x^2 + 4x - 8 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^4 - 3x^3 - x^2 + 3x = 0 \)
  4. \( x^4 - x^3 - 6x^2 + 6x = 0 \)

Verify solutions.

  1. \( x^3 - 2x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^3 - 3x^2 + 3x - 1 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^4 - 4x^2 = 0 \)
  4. \( x^4 + 2x^3 - x^2 - 2x = 0 \)

Level 4 — Full Structural Solving

Solve completely. Show all steps.

  1. \( x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^3 - 4x^2 - x + 4 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^3 - 7x^2 + 10x = 0 \)
  4. \( x^3 - 5x^2 + 6x = 0 \)
  5. \( x^4 - 6x^2 + 9 = 0 \)
  6. \( x^4 - 4x^2 = 0 \)
  7. \( x^4 - x^3 - 6x^2 + 6x = 0 \)

Solve and graph.

  1. \( x^3 - 2x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^3 - 3x^2 - 4x + 12 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^4 - 4x^2 = 0 \)
  4. \( x^3 - 3x^2 + 3x - 1 = 0 \)

Describe graph behavior at each solution.

  1. \( x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0 \)
  2. \( x^3 - 3x^2 + 3x - 1 = 0 \)
  3. \( x^3 - 2x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \)
  4. \( x^4 - 4x^2 = 0 \)

Level 5 — Justification

  1. Explain why factoring is required before applying the Zero Product Property.
  2. Explain how solutions relate to x-intercepts.
  3. Justify why synthetic division is useful for higher-degree polynomials.
  4. Explain how multiplicity affects graph behavior.
  5. Describe how algebraic and graphical solutions are connected.
