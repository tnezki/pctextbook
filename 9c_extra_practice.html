9.C Graph Rational Functions — Intercepts & Features

Extra Practice

Level 1 — Intercepts

Find x-intercepts

Set the numerator equal to zero.

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x-4}{x+2} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{3x+6}{x-1} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-9}{x+3} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{x(x+2)}{x-5} \)

Find y-intercepts

Substitute \(x=0\).

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x-3}{x+2} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{2x+5}{x-3} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-4}{x+1} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{4x}{x-2} \)

Determine if intercepts exist

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{1}{x-3} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{x}{x-1} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2+4}{x+2} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{0}{x+5} \)

Level 2 — Intercepts + Asymptotes

Find all intercepts and vertical asymptotes

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x-1}{x-4} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{x+2}{x^2-9} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-4}{x-3} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{3x}{x^2-4} \)

Find all asymptotes

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x}{x-2} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{3x^2}{x^2+5} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3}{x^2+2} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2+4}{x-2} \)

Identify all features

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x-2}{x+3} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-9}{x+2} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2}{x^2-4} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{3x+2}{x^2-9} \)

Level 3 — Holes & Structure

Simplify and identify all features

  1. \( \frac{x-3}{(x-3)(x+2)} \)
  2. \( \frac{x+4}{(x+4)(x-5)} \)
  3. \( \frac{x^2-4}{(x-2)(x+3)} \)
  4. \( \frac{x^2-16}{(x-4)(x+2)} \)

Find intercepts, asymptotes, and holes

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x(x-3)}{(x-3)(x+2)} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{(x+4)(x-2)}{(x+4)(x-5)} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-9}{x^2-4} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-4}{x^2-9} \)

Behavior near asymptotes

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{1}{x-3} \)
  2. \( f(x)=-\frac{2}{x+2} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{4}{x-5} \)
  4. \( f(x)=-\frac{3}{x-4} \)

Level 4 — Full Structural Analysis

Directions: Factor completely. Identify all features: x-intercepts, y-intercept, vertical asymptotes, horizontal/slant asymptotes, and any holes.

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-6x^2+11x-6}{x^2-5x+6} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-2x^2-5x+6}{x^2-1} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-5x^2+6x}{x^2-5x+6} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-4x^2-x+4}{x^2-4} \)
  5. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-1}{x^2-x-2} \)
  6. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-8}{x^2-4x+4} \)
  7. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-3x^2-4x+12}{x^2-4x+3} \)

Simplify, Then Analyze

Directions: Factor, simplify fully, then identify all features.

  1. \( \frac{x^3-4x^2+3x}{x^2-3x+2} \)
  2. \( \frac{x^3-5x^2+6x}{x^2-5x+6} \)
  3. \( \frac{x^3-9x}{x^2-9} \)
  4. \( \frac{x^3-2x^2-8x}{x^2-2x} \)

Level 5 — Justification

  1. Explain why x-intercepts come from the numerator.
  2. Explain why holes occur instead of asymptotes.
  3. Explain how asymptotes shape the graph.
  4. Explain why some rational functions have no intercepts.
  5. Explain how to fully analyze a rational function before graphing.
