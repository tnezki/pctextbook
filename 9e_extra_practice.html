9.E Evaluate Limits from Expressions

Extra Practice

Level 1 — Direct Substitution

Evaluate using direct substitution.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to -2} (x^3 + 2x) \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 5} (x^2 - 4x + 6) \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{3x+1}{2x+5} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 0} (4 - x^3) \)

Evaluate using substitution.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \sqrt{2x+8} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to -3} \frac{1}{x^2+1} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 2} (x^4 - 3x^2) \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x+2}{x+4} \)

Determine if the limit exists.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{x^3} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{1}{(x-2)^2} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to -1} (x+1)^3 \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x-3}{x-3} \)

Level 2 — Factoring

Evaluate by simplifying.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - 3x + 2}{x - 2} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^2 - 2x + 1}{x - 1} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to -2} \frac{x^2 + x - 2}{x + 2} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x^2 - x - 6}{x - 3} \)

Evaluate the limit (factor first).

  1. \( \lim_{x \to -1} \frac{x^2 + 4x + 3}{x + 1} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 - 6x + 8}{x - 4} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - 2x}{x - 2} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to -3} \frac{x^2 + 2x - 3}{x + 3} \)

Factor completely, then evaluate.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x^2 + 5x}{x} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^3 - 1}{x - 1} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to -2} \frac{x^3 + 8}{x + 2} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^3 - 8}{x - 2} \)

Level 3 — Rationalizing

Evaluate by rationalizing.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{x+1} - 1}{x} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{\sqrt{x+6} - 3}{x - 3} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 9} \frac{\sqrt{x+7} - 4}{x - 9} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{2 - \sqrt{x}}{x - 4} \)

Evaluate the limit.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 16} \frac{4 - \sqrt{x}}{16 - x} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\sqrt{2x+7} - 3}{x - 1} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{1+x} - 1}{x} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 25} \frac{5 - \sqrt{x}}{25 - x} \)

Verify algebraically.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{x+1} - 1}{x} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 16} \frac{4 - \sqrt{x}}{16 - x} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\sqrt{2x+7} - 3}{x - 1} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 9} \frac{\sqrt{x+7} - 4}{x - 9} \)

Level 4 — Complex Fractions

Simplify and evaluate.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\frac{x-1}{x}}{x-1} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{\frac{x^2-4}{x}}{x-2} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{\frac{x^2-9}{x+1}}{x-3} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x-1}{\frac{x^2-1}{x}} \)
  5. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{\frac{2}{x} - \frac{2}{3}}{x-3} \)
  6. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{\frac{x}{2} - 2}{x-4} \)
  7. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x-1}{\frac{1-x}{x}} \)

Evaluate and interpret.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^3 - 8}{x - 2} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 2}{x - 4} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^3 - 1}{x - 1} \)

Describe behavior near the limit.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{1}{x} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 0^-} \frac{1}{x} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{1}{(x-2)^2} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x^2 - 9}{x - 3} \)

Level 5 — Justification

  1. Explain why substitution can produce indeterminate forms.
  2. Explain why different algebraic techniques are needed.
  3. Justify why rationalizing removes radicals.
  4. Explain one-sided limits vs two-sided limits.
  5. Describe how limits connect algebra and graphs.
