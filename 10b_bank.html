Level 5 — Conceptual & Justification

  1. Logarithmic functions are undefined for nonpositive inputs, so the graph approaches a vertical asymptote where the argument equals zero.
  2. Exponential functions are defined for all real numbers, so no vertical asymptote exists.
  3. Horizontal shifts change where the argument equals zero, which shifts the vertical asymptote and domain.
  4. They undo each other; exponential growth corresponds to logarithmic scaling.
  5. Shifts move asymptotes, while reflections and stretches do not change their type.
