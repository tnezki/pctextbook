8.B
Operations with Rational Expressions
Whiteboard Partners
Whiteboard Partners
Setup: Work with your partner. One whiteboard and one marker between you.
  • Both partners stay engaged — no one is off-task at any point.
  • Take turns writing. Alternate who holds the marker each problem.
  • The partner who isn't writing checks each step and verifies the work.
  • Write large and clearly — your work should be readable from across the room.
  • Stay with your partner. No phones.
  • Use your resources: ask your partner first, then the teacher. Do not copy.
  • Mistakes are part of learning — fix them together and discuss what went wrong.
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Both partners actively writing or checking
  • Clear, large work visible from a distance
  • Talking through errors together before erasing
✗ What to Avoid
  • One person doing all the writing while the other watches
  • Work too small or messy to read from your seat
  • Copying from another group without understanding
Problem 1
Simplify and state all restrictions:
$$\frac{x^2 + 5x + 6}{x + 3}$$
Factor the numerator first.
Answer 1
$x^2 + 5x + 6 = (x+2)(x+3)$ $$\frac{(x+2)(x+3)}{x+3} = x + 2, \quad x \ne -3$$
Problem 2
Simplify and state all restrictions:
$$\frac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{x^2 - 9}$$
Factor both numerator and denominator.
Answer 2
$x^2 - 7x + 12 = (x-3)(x-4)$    $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$ $$\frac{x - 4}{x + 3}, \quad x \ne \pm 3$$
Problem 3
Simplify and state all restrictions:
$$\frac{x^2 + x - 6}{x^2 - 4}$$
Both are factorable — one is a trinomial, one is a difference of squares.
Answer 3
$x^2+x-6=(x+3)(x-2)$    $x^2-4=(x+2)(x-2)$ $$\frac{x + 3}{x + 2}, \quad x \ne \pm 2$$
Problem 4
Simplify and state all restrictions:
$$\frac{2x^2 + 7x + 3}{2x + 1}$$
Factor the numerator — leading coefficient is 2.
Answer 4
$2x^2 + 7x + 3 = (2x+1)(x+3)$ $$\frac{(2x+1)(x+3)}{2x+1} = x + 3, \quad x \ne -\tfrac{1}{2}$$
Problem 5
Multiply:
$$\frac{x^2 - x - 6}{x^2 - 9} \cdot \frac{x + 3}{x + 2}$$
Answer 5
$x^2-x-6=(x-3)(x+2)$    $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$ $$\frac{(x-3)(x+2)}{(x-3)(x+3)} \cdot \frac{x+3}{x+2} = 1, \quad x \ne \pm3,\,-2$$
Problem 6
Multiply:
$$\frac{x^2 + 3x - 10}{x^2 - 4} \cdot \frac{x - 2}{x + 5}$$
Answer 6
$x^2+3x-10=(x+5)(x-2)$    $x^2-4=(x+2)(x-2)$ $$\frac{(x+5)(x-2)}{(x+2)(x-2)} \cdot \frac{x-2}{x+5} = \frac{x-2}{x+2}, \quad x \ne \pm2,\,-5$$
Problem 7
Divide:
$$\frac{x^2 - 2x - 8}{x^2 - 16} \div \frac{x + 2}{x + 4}$$
Answer 7
$x^2-2x-8=(x-4)(x+2)$    $x^2-16=(x-4)(x+4)$ $$\frac{(x-4)(x+2)}{(x-4)(x+4)} \cdot \frac{x+4}{x+2} = 1, \quad x \ne \pm4,\,-2$$
Problem 8
Divide:
$$\frac{2x^2 + x - 6}{x^2 - 4} \div \frac{2x - 3}{x - 2}$$
Answer 8
$2x^2+x-6=(2x-3)(x+2)$    $x^2-4=(x+2)(x-2)$ $$\frac{(2x-3)(x+2)}{(x+2)(x-2)} \cdot \frac{x-2}{2x-3} = 1, \quad x \ne \pm2,\,\tfrac{3}{2}$$
Problem 9
Add:
$$\frac{3}{x^2 + 5x + 6} + \frac{1}{x + 2}$$
Factor the first denominator to find the LCD.
Answer 9
$x^2+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3)$    LCD $= (x+2)(x+3)$ $$\frac{3 + (x+3)}{(x+2)(x+3)} = \frac{x+6}{(x+2)(x+3)}, \quad x \ne -2,\,-3$$
Problem 10
Subtract:
$$\frac{x}{x^2 - x - 6} - \frac{2}{x - 3}$$
Factor the first denominator: it's a trinomial.
Answer 10
$x^2-x-6=(x-3)(x+2)$    LCD $= (x-3)(x+2)$ $$\frac{x - 2(x+2)}{(x-3)(x+2)} = \frac{-x-4}{(x-3)(x+2)}, \quad x \ne 3,\,-2$$
Problem 11
Add:
$$\frac{2}{x + 1} + \frac{x}{x^2 + 3x + 2}$$
Factor the second denominator to find the LCD.
Answer 11
$x^2+3x+2=(x+1)(x+2)$    LCD $= (x+1)(x+2)$ $$\frac{2(x+2) + x}{(x+1)(x+2)} = \frac{3x+4}{(x+1)(x+2)}, \quad x \ne -1,\,-2$$
Problem 12
Subtract:
$$\frac{4}{x - 2} - \frac{3}{x^2 - 5x + 6}$$
Factor the second denominator — it shares a factor with the first.
Answer 12
$x^2-5x+6=(x-2)(x-3)$    LCD $= (x-2)(x-3)$ $$\frac{4(x-3) - 3}{(x-2)(x-3)} = \frac{4x-15}{(x-2)(x-3)}, \quad x \ne 2,\,3$$
Problem 13
Simplify completely:
$$\frac{2x^2 + 5x - 3}{x^2 - 9} \cdot \frac{x + 3}{2x - 1}$$
Answer 13
$2x^2+5x-3=(2x-1)(x+3)$    $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$ $$\frac{(2x-1)(x+3)}{(x-3)(x+3)} \cdot \frac{x+3}{2x-1} = \frac{x+3}{x-3}, \quad x \ne \pm3,\,\tfrac{1}{2}$$
Problem 14
Simplify completely:
$$\frac{3x^2 - 5x - 2}{x^2 + x - 6} \div \frac{3x + 1}{x - 2}$$
Answer 14
$3x^2-5x-2=(3x+1)(x-2)$    $x^2+x-6=(x+3)(x-2)$ $$\frac{(3x+1)(x-2)}{(x+3)(x-2)} \cdot \frac{x-2}{3x+1} = \frac{x-2}{x+3}, \quad x \ne 2,\,-3,\,-\tfrac{1}{3}$$
Problem 15
Add:
$$\frac{x+1}{x^2 - x - 2} + \frac{2}{x^2 + x - 6}$$
Factor both denominators — they share a common factor.
Answer 15
$x^2-x-2=(x-2)(x+1)$    $x^2+x-6=(x-2)(x+3)$
LCD $= (x-2)(x+1)(x+3)$ $$\frac{(x+1)(x+3) + 2(x+1)}{(x-2)(x+1)(x+3)} = \frac{(x+1)(x+5)}{(x-2)(x+1)(x+3)}$$ $$= \frac{x+5}{(x-2)(x+3)}, \quad x \ne 2,\,-1,\,-3$$
Problem 16
Simplify completely:
$$\frac{x^2 + 2x - 15}{x^2 - 2x - 3} \div \frac{x^2 - 25}{x^2 + 4x + 3}$$
Answer 16
$x^2+2x-15=(x+5)(x-3)$    $x^2-2x-3=(x-3)(x+1)$
$x^2-25=(x+5)(x-5)$    $x^2+4x+3=(x+1)(x+3)$ $$\frac{(x+5)(x-3)}{(x-3)(x+1)} \cdot \frac{(x+1)(x+3)}{(x+5)(x-5)} = \frac{x+3}{x-5}$$ $$x \ne \pm5,\,3,\,-1,\,-3$$
