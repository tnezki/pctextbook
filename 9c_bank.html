9.C Graph Rational Functions — Intercepts & Features

Question Bank

Level 1 — Intercepts

Find x-intercepts

Set the numerator equal to zero.

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x-3}{x+1} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{2x+4}{x-5} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-4}{x+2} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{x(x-1)}{x+3} \)

Find y-intercepts

Substitute \(x=0\).

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x-2}{x+1} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{2x+3}{x-4} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-1}{x+2} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{3x}{x-1} \)

Determine if intercepts exist

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{1}{x+2} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{x}{x} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2+1}{x+3} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{0}{x-1} \)

Level 2 — Intercepts + Asymptotes

Find all intercepts and vertical asymptotes

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x-2}{x-3} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{x+1}{x^2-4} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-1}{x-2} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{2x}{x^2-1} \)

Find all asymptotes

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x}{x+1} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{2x^2}{x^2+3} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3}{x^2+1} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2+1}{x-1} \)

Identify all features

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x-1}{x+2} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-4}{x+1} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2}{x^2-1} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{2x+1}{x^2-4} \)

Level 3 — Holes & Structure

Simplify and identify all features

  1. \( \frac{x-2}{(x-2)(x+1)} \)
  2. \( \frac{x+3}{(x+3)(x-4)} \)
  3. \( \frac{x^2-1}{(x-1)(x+2)} \)
  4. \( \frac{x^2-9}{(x-3)(x+1)} \)

Find intercepts, asymptotes, and holes

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x(x-2)}{(x-2)(x+1)} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{(x+3)(x-1)}{(x+3)(x-4)} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-4}{x^2-1} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{x^2-1}{x^2-4} \)

Behavior near asymptotes

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{1}{x-2} \)
  2. \( f(x)=-\frac{1}{x+1} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{2}{x-3} \)
  4. \( f(x)=-\frac{3}{x-2} \)

Level 4 — Full Structural Analysis (Advanced)

Identify ALL features

Directions: Factor completely. Identify all features: x-intercepts, y-intercept, vertical asymptotes, horizontal/slant asymptotes, and any holes.

  1. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-6x^2+11x-6}{x^2-5x+6} \)
  2. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-3x^2-4x+12}{x^2-4} \)
  3. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-4x}{x^2-5x+6} \)
  4. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-7x^2+10x}{x^2-3x} \)
  5. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-1}{x^2-x-2} \)
  6. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-8}{x^2-4x+4} \)
  7. \( f(x)=\frac{x^3-5x^2+6x}{x^2-4x+3} \)

Simplify, Then Analyze

  1. \( \frac{x^3-3x^2+2x}{x^2-3x+2} \)

  3. \( \frac{x^3-2x^2-15x+18}{x^2-9} \)

Behavior Near Asymptotes
