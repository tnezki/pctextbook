9.E Evaluate Limits from Expressions

Question Bank

Level 1 — Direct Substitution

Evaluate using direct substitution.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 2} (x^2 + 5) \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to -1} (3x + 4) \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 4} (x^2 - x + 1) \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 3} (2x^2 - 5x + 1) \)

Evaluate using substitution.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 9} \sqrt{x + 7} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{1}{x+2} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to -2} (x^3 + 3x^2) \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 0} (5 - x^2) \)

Determine if the limit exists.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{1}{x-2} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{x} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 3} (x^2 - 9) \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to -1} (x+1)^2 \)

Level 2 — Factoring

Evaluate by simplifying.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - 5x + 6}{x - 2} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x^2 - 4x + 3}{x - 3} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to -1} \frac{x^2 + 3x + 2}{x + 1} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 - 6x + 8}{x - 4} \)

Evaluate the limit.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^2 + x - 2}{x - 1} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to -2} \frac{x^2 + x - 6}{x + 2} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - x - 2}{x - 2} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to -3} \frac{x^2 + 5x + 6}{x + 3} \)

Factor completely, then evaluate.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - 5x + 6}{x - 2} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{x - 3} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to -1} \frac{x^2 + 4x + 3}{x + 1} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 - 3x - 4}{x - 4} \)

Level 3 — Rationalizing

Evaluate by rationalizing.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 2}{x - 4} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\sqrt{x+3} - 2}{x - 1} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 9} \frac{3 - \sqrt{x}}{9 - x} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 16} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 4}{x - 16} \)

Evaluate the limit.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{x+4} - 2}{x} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 1}{x - 1} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 25} \frac{5 - \sqrt{x}}{25 - x} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{2 - \sqrt{x}}{4 - x} \)

Verify the result.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 2}{x - 4} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 9} \frac{3 - \sqrt{x}}{9 - x} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 1}{x - 1} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 16} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 4}{x - 16} \)

Level 4 — Complex Fractions

Simplify and evaluate.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\frac{1}{x} - 1}{x - 1} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{2}}{x - 2} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{3}}{x - 3} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{\frac{x}{4} - 1}{x - 4} \)
  5. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{\frac{2}{x} - 1}{x - 2} \)
  6. \( \lim_{x \to 5} \frac{\frac{x}{5} - 1}{x - 5} \)
  7. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x - 1}{\frac{1}{x} - 1} \)

Evaluate and interpret.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 2}{x - 4} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\frac{1}{x} - 1}{x - 1} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x^2 - 9}{x - 3} \)

Describe behavior near the limit.

  1. \( \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{1}{x - 2} \)
  2. \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{x} \)
  3. \( \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x^2 - 9}{x - 3} \)
  4. \( \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{\sqrt{x} - 2}{x - 4} \)

Level 5 — Justification

  1. Explain why direct substitution sometimes fails.
  2. Explain why factoring removes indeterminate forms.
  3. Justify why rationalizing works.
  4. Explain how limits describe behavior near discontinuities.
  5. Describe how algebra and graphs represent limits.
