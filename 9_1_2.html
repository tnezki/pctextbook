Abe, Barturi, and Cassie work at Budding Success Flower Shop. Each day, they plan to make bouquets in three styles in quantities indicated by matrix \(E\), an employees-by-bouquet styles matrix, shown at right.

\(E=\begin{bmatrix}4 & 8 & 3 \\ 6 & 4 & 7 \\ 4 & 8 & 5 \\ 5 & 6 & 6\end{bmatrix}\)

  1. How many #2 bouquets will Cassie make each day?

  2. Who makes the most bouquets?

  3. Write a matrix that shows how many bouquet styles each worker makes in one full workweek (Monday through Friday).

  4. Represent your answer to part (c) in terms of \(E\).
